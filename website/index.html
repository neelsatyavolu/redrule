How it works

  1. 1

    Take the call as usual

    Redrule sits in the menu bar and offers to record when a Zoom or Google Meet call starts. For any other call, record it from the menu bar.

  2. 2

    It writes as people talk

    Speech is transcribed live on your Mac by NVIDIA's Parakeet, one of the most accurate open speech models. Your microphone is always Me, and the other voices on the call are told apart.

  3. 3

    Notes when you hang up

    A summary, the decisions and the action items, written on your Mac or by the ChatGPT or Grok account you connect. Then ask anything about the meeting, tick items off, and tag meetings to find them later.

Questions

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. Redrule records the call's sound on your Mac, the way you hear it. Nobody else sees anything join.

Do I need a subscription or an API key?

No. Redrule is free. Notes can be written on your Mac with no account at all, or you can sign in once to the ChatGPT or Grok plan you already have. Transcription always runs on your Mac.

Should notes be written on my Mac or with ChatGPT or Grok?

On your Mac, nothing leaves it and nothing needs a connection. Qwen3.5 4B writes a meeting's notes in a minute or two and stays light on memory and battery; the 9B model is sharper on long or technical meetings, about twice as slow, and needs 16 GB of memory. ChatGPT and Grok are faster, and the transcript goes to your account. You can switch at any time in Settings.

Can I ask questions about a meeting?

Yes. Once a meeting ends, ask anything about it and follow up. Answers come from its notes and transcript, from the model on your Mac or your ChatGPT or Grok account, whichever you choose in Settings.

How accurate is the transcript?

Very good on clear audio. Parakeet ranks among the best open speech models on public benchmarks. Like any transcription it can mishear names, jargon, cross-talk and poor connections. You can rename speakers and edit the notes afterwards.

Does it work offline?

Yes, when notes are written on your Mac: recording, transcription and notes all work without a connection. With ChatGPT or Grok, recording and transcription still work offline; choose Write notes once you're back online.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple Silicon running macOS 15 or later. On first launch Redrule asks for microphone and system audio access and downloads its speech model once. Writing notes on your Mac adds a one-time download of 2.7 GB, or 5.7 GB for the larger model.

How do updates work?

Redrule checks for new versions in the background and installs them itself. It never restarts during a recording. You can also choose Check for Updates… from the menu.
