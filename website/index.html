How it works

  1. 1

    Take the call as usual

    Redrule sits in the menu bar and offers to record when a Zoom or Google Meet call starts. For any other call, record it from the menu bar.

  2. 2

    It writes as people talk

    Speech is transcribed live on your Mac by NVIDIA's Parakeet, one of the most accurate open speech models. Your microphone is always Me, and the other voices on the call are told apart.

  3. 3

    Notes when you hang up

    A summary, the decisions and the action items, written by the ChatGPT or Grok account you connect. Tick items off, rename speakers, edit anything.

Questions

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. Redrule records the call's sound on your Mac, the way you hear it. Nobody else sees anything join.

Do I need another subscription or an API key?

No. Redrule is free and uses the ChatGPT or Grok plan you already have: you sign in once in Settings. Transcription runs on your Mac and needs no account at all.

How accurate is the transcript?

Very good on clear audio. Parakeet ranks among the best open speech models on public benchmarks. Like any transcription it can mishear names, jargon, cross-talk and poor connections, so every transcript and note can be edited.

Does it work offline?

Recording and transcription do. Writing the notes needs your connected AI account, so notes are written once you're back online.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple Silicon running macOS 15 or later. On first launch Redrule asks for microphone and system audio access and downloads its speech model once.

How do updates work?

Redrule checks for new versions in the background and installs them itself. It never restarts during a recording. You can also choose Check for Updates… from the menu.
